## 1. Chart of Accounts Management

- Design account hierarchy matching your business structure
- Unlimited accounts for detailed tracking
- Bulk import from Excel for quick setup
- Auto-complete search for fast entry

## 2. Journal System (5 Types)

**Sales Journal (SJ)**
- Record project billings and revenue
- Track payment terms
- Link to customers/projects

**Cash Receipt Journal (CR)**
- Customer payments tracking
- Bank deposit reconciliation
- Apply payments to invoices

**Accounts Payable Journal (APJ)**
- Vendor invoice tracking
- Payment due dates
- Manage payables by vendor

**Disbursement Journal (DJ)**
- Check register functionality
- Vendor payments
- Expense tracking

**General Journal (GJ)**
- Adjusting entries
- Depreciation
- Corrections and transfers

## 3. Financial Reports

**Trial Balance**
- Verify double-entry accuracy
- Month-end closing support
- Export to Excel

**General Ledger**
- Transaction-level detail
- Running balances
- Drill-down capabilities

**Income Statement**
- Revenue and expense analysis
- Gross profit calculation
- Net income tracking

**Balance Sheet**
- Assets, liabilities, equity
- Financial position snapshot
- Loan application support

## 4. Construction-Specific Features

**Vendor Management**

- TIN tracking for compliance

**Customer Management**

- Credit terms tracking

- Equipment purchases

## 5. User Management and Security

- Unlimited users - No per-user fees

- Secure authentication - Password protection

## 6. Audit and Compliance

- Approval workflows - Draft, Submit, Approve
