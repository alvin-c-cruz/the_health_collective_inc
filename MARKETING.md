## 💼 Business Workflows

### **Daily Operations Flow**

```
Morning:
1. Staff creates transactions throughout the day (DRAFT status)
2. Staff reviews and submits transactions for approval
3. Supervisor/Admin approves transactions
4. Transactions become POSTED and generate accounting entries

End of Day:
5. Staff prepares bank deposit with cash transactions
6. Submit deposit for approval
7. Admin approves deposit
8. Bank deposit reconciles to actual bank transaction

Collections:
9. Review outstanding receivables
10. Record customer payments
11. Apply payments to specific invoices
12. Submit collection for approval
```

### **Month-End Closing Flow**

```
1. Review all pending approvals and complete
2. Generate Trial Balance to verify accounts
3. Review General Ledger for accuracy
4. Make adjusting entries in General Journal
5. Run Income Statement and Balance Sheet
6. Export reports for tax filing
7. Archive monthly reports
```

### **APE Batch Processing Flow**

```
Corporate Health Checkups:
1. Create APE batch for company contract
2. Record individual patient transactions linked to batch
3. Batch automatically tracks total transactions and amounts
4. Generate Statement of Account for batch
5. Record collection against batch
6. Monitor outstanding balance per batch
```
